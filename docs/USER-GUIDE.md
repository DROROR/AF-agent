# DYO Video Agent — how to use it

## What it is

It takes an After Effects template you already own, puts your content into it, and renders two finished videos: one landscape and one vertical (1080×1920).

It does **not** invent video. It fills in a designer's template and never changes the original file.

## What the AI does — and what it doesn't

**It suggests.** Which of your files or texts belongs in which slot, with its reasoning. Nothing it suggests is ever applied on its own; you accept or reject each one.

**It never** runs anything in After Effects, decides what gets rendered, or approves its own work. Every real edit and every render is a fixed, pre-approved operation that a person signed off first.

Where the system measures things — slot shapes, when a slot is on screen, the vertical layout — that is deterministic geometry, not a guess, and you can always overrule it.

## The steps

Each project walks the same path. The page always tells you which step you are on and which button to press next.

1. **Upload** — your template, then your images, logo and any video.
2. **Match your content** — review each scene. Anything uncertain is blocked until you look at a real frame and decide. That is deliberate.
3. **Approve the plan.**
4. **First preview** — one designed frame. Check it looks right, approve it.
5. **Render settings** — pick which composition becomes the landscape master, and the vertical one if you want Reels. *Set the Reels layout before approving the plan, or the approvals reset.*
6. **Complete preview** — the whole video. Approve it.
7. **Export** — render and download both versions.

## Before every session

**Open After Effects and leave it empty** — no project loaded, no dialog on screen. The system cannot work while After Effects is waiting for a click, and it will never answer a "Save changes?" prompt for you.

## Things that will stop you, and why

**A greyed-out button** always says why it is greyed out and what unlocks it. Read the line under it.

**A blocked slot** means the system is not confident enough to act. Capture the frame, look at it, then decide. It will not guess on your behalf.

**"Not measured" on the Reels layout** usually means the editing computer is on an older build.

**Fonts** must be installed on the editing computer. A missing font is silently substituted — the render succeeds and looks wrong. Check the fonts a template needs before you promise a result.

## What it cannot do yet

- **Add your own music.** A template's own audio renders fine, but supplying your own soundtrack is not built yet.
- **Reposition animated layers for vertical.** A layer with its own animation is left where the designer put it rather than having its animation destroyed — it says which layers those are.

## If something goes wrong

Nothing is lost. Every step keeps its own record, the original template is never modified, and an interrupted job is picked up and reported rather than left hanging. Tell us what the screen said — the wording is specific on purpose.
